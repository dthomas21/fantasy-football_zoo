Rules & Prizes

The keeper rule, the lottery odds, and what you're actually playing for.

The Keeper Rule

One keeper per team, held for an unlimited number of seasons. A player qualifies if he meets any of these:

  • Carries a Madden rating of 89 overall or less on the current season's rankings. Only the first list released to the public counts.
  • Is a rookie entering his first NFL season. Rookies are exempt from the Madden rule entirely.
  • Was your keeper last season. Keeping him again needs no Madden check — but the exemption only carries from the year immediately before. Drop him for a season and it is gone.

The same rules apply regardless of how you got the player — draft, trade, or waivers. Keepers must be on your roster at season's end. Offseason trades are encouraged.

Publishing the ineligible list each year falls to Commissioner Tiny. Should Madden ever stop production, the league votes on new keeper parameters.

Lottery Odds

Two different yardsticks, depending on how your season ended.

  • Winners bracket (top 4). Seeded by where you finished. 1st place draws the worst odds.
  • Everyone else (5th–8th). Seeded by overall record including playoffs, best to worst — each playoff matchup counts as one game even though it runs two weeks.
